# Initial Opportunity Comparison

Research date: 2026-07-27 CEST

Decision scope: ways to turn 200 SEK into **settled, withdrawable cash by
2026-08-26 23:11:10 CEST**. Revenue that would arrive after the deadline is
worth zero for this decision.

## Scoring method

Each factor is scored from 0 to 5. The weighted total is out of 100:

- expected settled profit: 30%
- probability of at least one success: 20%
- speed to settled cash: 15%
- budget safety: 10%
- scalability inside 30 days: 10%
- low required human work: 10%
- low legal/platform risk: 5%

The cash estimates are decision estimates, not promises. They combine the
observed price/payout evidence below with conservative assumptions for a new,
unrated seller.

## 1. Productized accessibility risk scan — selected

### Current demand and buyer

- The Swedish Post and Telecom Authority (PTS) started e-commerce accessibility
  supervision and says reviews continue during 2025 and 2026. It is examining
  whether sites are perceivable, operable, understandable, and robust:
  <https://pts.se/nyheter-och-pressmeddelanden/pts-inleder-en-tillsyn-av-e-handelstjanster/>
- PTS's 2026 report measured 576 Swedish sites and about 26,500 pages. It found
  almost two automatically detected deficiencies per page in the private sector,
  and says 83–100% of sites in every measured sector had deficiencies capable
  of excluding blind users. It also warns that automation misses important
  problems, so the true share with barriers is probably higher:
  <https://www.pts.se/contentassets/c092a37ebe5a4a18af99ea151afb2fbc/tillgangligheten-for-svenska-webbplatser-slutversion.pdf>
- PTS says e-commerce supervision can lead to remedial orders or sanction fees.
  It also says micro-enterprises providing services are exempt and that
  automatic tools alone have limitations:
  <https://pts.se/digital-inkludering/lagen-om-vissa-produkters-och-tjansters-tillganglighet/vanliga-fragor-och-svar-om-tillganglighetslagen/>
- Current marketplace evidence supports paid audit demand. Upwork displayed 236
  accessibility-testing jobs on 2026-07-27. Comparable postings include a
  $250 paid single-page test leading to $800–$1,500 projects, and a $750 audit
  and report:
  <https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/accessibility/>
  <https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/apply/Section-508-Accessibility-Specialist-Long-Term-Partnership-VPAT-Experience-Preferred_~022012286371967863022/>
  <https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/apply/Accessibility-Consultant-for-WCAG-Audit-and-Report_~022011238163609349279/>

Likely buyer: the person responsible for e-commerce, digital experience, or the
website at a Swedish consumer e-commerce company that is not obviously a
micro-enterprise.

### Offer, reach, price, cost, and settlement

- Pilot offer: **1,900 SEK excluding VAT**, paid before delivery.
- Deliverable within two business days: homepage, one product page, and one
  search/checkout-flow page; automated checks plus manual keyboard, zoom,
  structure, form-label, and interaction checks; prioritized findings; code-level
  remediation guidance; a 20-minute handoff.
- It is explicitly a risk scan, not legal advice, certification, or a guarantee
  of compliance. That matches PTS's warning that automated tools alone cannot
  establish accessibility.
- Buyer acquisition: 15–25 individually researched Swedish e-commerce companies,
  using public corporate role addresses/contact forms only. Each message will
  contain one or two verified observations, identify the commercial sender, and
  offer a simple opt-out. No mass mail or personal scraped list.
- Cash cost: 0 SEK for the first demand test. Free open-source tools and manual
  testing are sufficient. Preserve the 200 SEK unless a validated acquisition
  channel justifies spending.
- Settlement: require prepayment or immediate card/Swish payment. A normal
  30-day invoice could miss the deadline and is therefore not the default.

### Competition, burden, and risk

Specialist agencies and certified auditors have stronger credentials. The pilot
competes on a tightly limited scope, fast turnaround, plain Swedish, developer-
ready fixes, and a low trial price—not on a false claim of full certification.
Delivery is feasible without customer credentials because the first product is
limited to public pages. The main risk is acquiring trust as a new seller.

The legal guardrails are strict:

- never imply that PTS selected or endorsed the prospect;
- never claim a legal violation from an automated scan;
- exclude an entity from law-based messaging unless its non-micro status is
  supported by public evidence;
- stop direct marketing on objection. IMY says direct marketing may be a
  legitimate interest but processing must stop when the person objects:
  <https://www.imy.se/verksamhet/dataskydd/det-har-galler-enligt-gdpr/rattslig-grund/intresseavvagning/>
- Swedish marketing law requires prior consent for electronic marketing to a
  natural person and a valid address for stopping messages. Outreach therefore
  uses corporate role channels rather than personal addresses:
  <https://lagrum.se/sfs/2008%3A486/P19>
  <https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/departementsserien/battre-regler-for-elektroniska-kommunikationer_GYB419/html/>

### Expected value

For planning, 20 highly tailored messages at an assumed 1.5% independent
conversion rate imply about a 26% chance of at least one sale
(`1 - 0.985^20`). One net 1,900 SEK sale gives roughly 494 SEK probability-
weighted revenue before payment fees and VAT liability. The recorded range is
lower (350–600 SEK) to reflect dependence between prospects, trust risk, and the
possibility of more than one sale. These rates must be replaced by actual funnel
data after the first batch.

## 2. Fixed-scope marketplace contracts

Upwork currently shows real automation demand: 2,478 jobs on the automation
category page, including a $250 GoHighLevel/n8n integration posted on
2026-07-27. That job already had 20–50 proposals and came from a same-day client,
illustrating both demand and competition:
<https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/automation/>
<https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/apply/GHL-n8n-Automation-Build-for-Home-Services-Agency-with-Walkthrough_~022081580414950497212/>

Upwork charges freelancers 0–15% by contract, most proposals require paid
Connects at $0.15 each, and fixed-price money has a five-day hold after approval:
<https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062538-Learn-about-the-Freelancer-Service-Fee>
<https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062898-Understanding-and-using-Connects>
<https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211063718-How-payments-for-milestones-and-fixed-price-contracts-work>

This is a strong secondary channel after a portfolio artifact exists. A new
profile, identity/tax setup, Connect costs, and proposal competition lower the
initial probability. It requires human authorization for an identity-bound
account and terms.

## 3. Open-source software bounties

Algora advertises 100% of contributor earnings and 2–7 day payouts:
<https://algora.io/pricing/>

However, the live Cal.com bounty board showed only two open entries for the same
issue, both already claimed; one was 38 months old. That makes immediately
claimable inventory too weak for a primary strategy:
<https://algora.io/cal/bounties?status=open>

No budget is at risk, so this remains a passive backup if a genuinely current,
unclaimed, well-scoped bounty appears.

## 4. Digital download

There is visible demand but weak evidence for a new seller. Etsy showed 261
results for WCAG, with checklists mostly around £2–£18; one recent Notion
accessibility tracker had only one sale, and a 2026 shop dedicated to accessible
documents showed zero sales:
<https://www.etsy.com/market/wcag>
<https://www.etsy.com/uk/listing/4501842209/ada-website-compliance-checklist-wcag>
<https://www.etsy.com/nz/shop/AccessibleDocuments>

Etsy charges $0.20 per listing and 6.5% per transaction, plus payment-processing
and possible advertising/setup fees:
<https://www.etsy.com/se-en/legal/fees>

Gumroad has no monthly fee but charges 10% + $0.50 plus processing on direct
sales and 30% on marketplace discovery. It holds sales at least seven days,
requires a $100 payout balance, and says first review can take 1–3 weeks:
<https://gumroad.com/help/article/66-gumroads-fees.html>
<https://gumroad.com/help/article/13-getting-paid>

The product is cheap to make and scalable, but discovery and payout thresholds
make realized cash within 30 days unlikely without an existing audience.

## 5. Micro-SaaS

A monitor or report generator could later support the service, but selling a
subscription before the deadline adds hosting, payments, support, privacy, and
acquisition burden. Stripe's Swedish price is 1.5% + 1.80 SEK for standard EEA
cards. Vercel Hobby is explicitly non-commercial and Pro is $20/month—about the
entire race budget. Cloudflare Workers has a free tier, but distribution remains
the constraint:
<https://stripe.com/en-se/pricing>
<https://vercel.com/docs/plans/hobby>
<https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/>

The audit tooling will be designed for later reuse, but no standalone SaaS will
be built before service demand is validated.

## 6. Affiliate content

Amazon Sweden pays about 60 days after the end of the earning month. Even a
July conversion would normally pay at the end of September, after the race:
<https://affiliate-program.amazon.se/help/node/topic/G63DR893K4DH55XZ>

Therefore all affiliate revenue initiated now has zero realized-cash value for
this race and the route is rejected.

## 7. Inventory arbitrage

Tradera provides fast settlement—manual Swish payout can be immediate—and the
first 500 listings are free to post:
<https://www.tradera.com/support/se/posts/ditt-saldo-och-utbetalningar/>
<https://www.tradera.com/support/se/>

But 200 SEK cannot buy enough diversified inventory; platform fees, shipping,
mispricing, and unsold inventory can consume the score. It also requires human
procurement, photography, packing, and shipping, which is disproportionate to
the plausible 0–50 SEK profit. Existing personal inventory is not used because
it would be an unpriced subsidy.

## 8. Participant research/testing

Prolific has a waitlist and ID verification, requires at least $/£6 to cash out,
and enforces minimum pay of $8/£6 per hour:
<https://www.prolific.com/participants-how-it-works>

UserTesting pays 14 days after a completed test:
<https://help.usertesting.com/hc/en-us/articles/11880240002845-Participant-compensation>

The contestant cannot honestly provide a human participant's opinions. Asking
the administrator to perform studies would consume the race's scarce human-time
allowance as substitute revenue labor. It is therefore rejected even though the
cash settlement itself can be fast.

## Decision

Build and sell the 1,900 SEK accessibility risk scan first. Use the completed
sample and audit tool as portfolio evidence for a secondary Upwork push if
direct B2B outreach is declined or produces no replies after the first measured
batch. Spend zero SEK until an actual channel constraint or conversion signal
supports a purchase.
