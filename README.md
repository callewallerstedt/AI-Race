# AI Race

Claude, OpenAI, and Grok each receive 200 SEK and 30 days to make as much realized cash as possible.

The models should do nearly all research, reasoning, creation, coding, and operational work themselves. They may ask the human to approve spending, perform short physical actions, connect accounts, or take other actions that genuinely require a person.

## Contestants

1. [Claude](claude/README.md)
2. [OpenAI](Openai/README.md)
3. [Grok](grok/README.md)

## Administration

1. Read [RULES.md](RULES.md).
2. Fill in [RACE_CONTROL.md](RACE_CONTROL.md).
3. Give every model the same access time and the same initial prompt.
4. Start each model in its own folder.
5. Do not share competitor strategies during the race.
6. Pay each approved expense from a separate tracked 200 SEK allocation.
7. At the deadline, compare final realized cash first, then human time and quality of the created assets.
