# Opportunity comparison — Grok

Race window: 2026-07-27 → 2026-08-26 (Europe/Stockholm). Starting cash: 200.00 SEK.
Score = unspent budget + settled/withdrawable revenue − liabilities. Inventory and unpaid invoices count as zero.

## Method

Investigated five materially different opportunities with current public evidence (July 2026 sources). Ranked on expected profit in 30 days, probability of ≥1 settled sale, downside to the 200 SEK, speed to cash, scalability, human-minute dependence, and legal/platform risk.

---

## Opportunity 1 — Generic Etsy digital printables / planners

**Buyer:** Global Etsy shoppers searching planners, wall art, Canva kits.
**Path:** Create listings → organic Etsy search.
**Price:** Typically USD 3–25.
**Cost:** USD 0.20 listing fee each + design time; Canva free tier possible.
**Time to settled cash:** New-seller payout holds often 14–20 days after first sale; bank transfer extra days.
**Competition:** Very high; generic planners saturated (2026 seller guides).
**Ops burden:** Many listings needed; SEO copy; customer messages.
**Legal:** Standard marketplace terms; original designs required.

**Verdict:** Weak for this race. Listing fees spend scarce capital; discovery without reviews is slow; payout lag threatens deadline scoring.

---

## Opportunity 2 — Affiliate content (Amazon.se / Adtraction)

**Buyer:** Consumers clicking through review/comparison content.
**Path:** Build pages → SEO/social → affiliate cookies → purchase.
**Price:** Commission 1–12% typical (Amazon category-dependent); networks vary.
**Cost:** Near zero cash if self-hosted static pages; time-heavy.
**Time to settled cash:** Networks often pay monthly with thresholds; SEO rarely converts inside 30 days without an existing audience.
**Competition:** High for commercial keywords.
**Ops burden:** Continuous content; disclosure law in Sweden.
**Legal:** Must disclose ads; Amazon Associates policy.

**Verdict:** Rejected as primary. Rules forbid using private audiences unless shared to all contestants. Cold SEO cannot reliably settle cash inside the window.

---

## Opportunity 3 — Fiverr productized gigs (web/dev microservices)

**Buyer:** Global Fiverr purchasers.
**Path:** Seller account → gigs → orders → delivery → clearance → withdraw.
**Price:** Entry packages often USD 20–100+; 20% seller commission.
**Cost:** Free to list.
**Time to settled cash:** 14 days clearance after order completion for new sellers (Fiverr Help), plus withdrawal time. Need completion ~by mid-August to score.
**Competition:** Severe for new accounts with zero reviews.
**Ops burden:** High response-time expectations; delivery SLAs.
**Legal:** Platform ToS; tax on income.

**Verdict:** Viable backup channel, not primary. Clearance clock + cold-start make settled cash fragile inside 30 days.

---

## Opportunity 4 — Swedish productized web micro-service (direct)

**Buyer:** Swedish sole traders / micro-SMEs with weak or missing one-page sites.
**Path:** Portfolio demos → targeted outreach / free boards → fixed packages → Swish or Frilans Finans.
**Price:** Planned packages 499 / 999 / 1799 SEK.
**Cost:** 0 SEK to build (static demos + copy). Payment rails free to register; Frilans Finans takes ~4–6% on invoiced amount when used.
**Time to settled cash:** Swish via Frilans Finans: often next-day salary path per their public materials; invoice path ~5 bank days. Fits deadline if sold by ~week 3.
**Competition:** Agencies are expensive (freelance landing builds often hundreds–thousands USD internationally); local DIY is the real competitor.
**Ops burden:** Delivery of 1–2 pages is fully automatable by the AI contestant; human needed for identity, payment account, and sending outreach.
**Legal:** Honest claims only; egenanställning handles tax remittance if used; no fake reviews.

**Verdict:** Strongest primary for race scoring. High ticket means 1 settled job beats dozens of low digital downloads. Fast settlement options exist in Sweden.

---

## Opportunity 5 — Niche digital product: Swedish freelancer finance workbook

**Buyer:** Swedish freelancers / egenanställda who price jobs across platforms and track cash without pretending Excel is legal bokföring.
**Path:** Build .xlsx → sell via Gumroad or Payhip → promote in freelancing communities with approval.
**Price:** 149 SEK (or 199 SEK).
**Cost:** 0 SEK to create; Gumroad ~10% / Payhip free plan ~5% + processing.
**Time to settled cash:** Payhip+Stripe often faster; Gumroad has ≥7-day hold, weekly SE payouts, new-account review can take 1–3 weeks, and ~USD 100 minimum — **Gumroad alone is dangerous for deadline scoring**. Prefer Payhip or direct Swish for this race.
**Competition:** Many free invoice/budget templates exist; differentiation = platform fee logic (Fiverr/Gumroad/Frilans Finans), settlement tracking, pricing calculator, explicit non-bokföring framing.
**Ops burden:** Low after build; support questions possible.
**Legal:** Must not claim to be compliant bookkeeping (BFN: Excel not allowed for löpande bokföring).

**Verdict:** Strong secondary / hedge. Zero marginal cost. Build fully now. Sell through rails with fast settlement.

## Selected strategy

**Primary:** Sell fixed-scope Swedish web micro-services (Webbfix Mini 499 SEK, Landing Basic 999 SEK, Landing Pro 1799 SEK) with portfolio demos already built. Payment preference: Frilans Finans Swish / invoice for settlement speed.

**Secondary:** Sell `Svensk-Frilans-Ekonomi-Pack.xlsx` at 149 SEK via a fast-settlement storefront (Payhip preferred over Gumroad for this race).

**Capital policy:** Spend 0 SEK until a purchase has clear positive EV and human approval. Prefer free rails.
