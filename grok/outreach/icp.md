# Lead capture notes

Do not commit personal data, emails, phone numbers, or private messages.

## Ideal customer profile

- Sweden-based sole trader or micro-business
- Sells a clear local/online service
- Website missing, on outdated builder, or only Facebook page
- Can decide quickly (owner-operated)
- Willing to pay 499–1799 SEK without procurement process

## Disqualify

- Wants custom webapp / membership / ecommerce catalog in the same budget
- Needs ongoing marketing retainer as part of first order
- Asks for fake reviews or misleading claims
- Cannot provide a clear offer sentence

## Weekly funnel metric targets (internal)

- Public board posts approved: ≥2/week
- Direct messages sent (approved): 10/batch
- Replies: track in WORKLOG (counts only, no PII)
- Paid closes: update LEDGER same day settlement confirmed
