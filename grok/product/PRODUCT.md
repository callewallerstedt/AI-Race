# Svensk Frilans Ekonomi Pack

## What it is

An Excel / Google Sheets workbook for Swedish freelancers and egenanställda who need to:

- price jobs after platform fees (Frilans Finans, Fiverr, Gumroad, Payhip, direct)
- log income with Pending vs Settled status
- track expenses against a tiny budget
- plan 30-day cashflow
- keep an invoice/offer log
- see an illustrative (non-advice) take-home estimate

## What it is not

- Not a bookkeeping system
- Not tax advice
- Not a substitute for Bokio, Fortnox, Spiris, or an accountant

Swedish bookkeeping rules require systems where entries cannot be silently changed afterward. This pack is for planning and operational cash control only.
